The Strategy

BUILT ON
DIVERGENCE

Every trade is based on a carefully selected divergence setup. No random entries, no FOMO trades — just high-probability setups with defined criteria.

🎯
THE HPS TRADE
The core setup — a divergence with a steep angle between stage one and stage two. Entry timing is based on the 5-minute timeframe, with trend and news as additional factors.

Entry: 5-min divergence confirmation
Contracts: 3–5 per trade
MES profit range: $20 – $100
ES profit range: $200 – $1,000

📊
CONTRACT OPTIONS
Trades execute on MES (Micro E-mini S&P) by default. Participants can switch to ES (E-mini S&P) through the Replikanto software for larger position sizing.

Default: MES (Micro E-mini)
Optional: ES with crossover switch
Recommended: Activate crossover for ES minis

⚠️
NEWS EVENT PROTOCOL
Efforts are made to avoid trading during major news events — unless there is a clear, high-level divergence opportunity that meets all criteria. This discipline protects your account from erratic price spikes and ensures every trade aligns with the system's core methodology.
Qualifying Setups

TRADE CRITERIA

Only these specific patterns qualify for execution. Every trade must meet the strict entry rules — no exceptions.

📉
HPS DIVERGENCE
The primary setup — steep angle divergence between stage 1 and stage 2 on the 5-minute chart. Both long and short sides.
🔄
1-2-3 CHANNEL + QUAD ROTATION
Channel breakout combined with the quad rotation signal. Taken when aligned with the dominant trend direction.
🌀
COILED STOCHASTIC
Less common but high-conviction. Taken especially when it ends in a divergence, amplifying the probability of success.
🏁
20/20 BULL FLAG
A perfect bull flag pattern taken on occasion when the strength of the current uptrend supports the setup.
🐻
20/20 BEAR FLAG
The bearish counterpart — a perfect bear flag taken when the current downtrend has clear momentum and structure.
💎
STRICT CRITERIA ONLY
No random trades, no FOMO entries. If the setup doesn't meet every rule, it doesn't fire. Discipline is the edge.
